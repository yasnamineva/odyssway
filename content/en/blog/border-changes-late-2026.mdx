Most border rules stay the same for years. A few changed this autumn, and
three end-of-year deadlines could change your plans for trips in early 2027.
Each item below links to the page with the full rule, its official source
and the date we checked it.

**Short version:** budget for Kenya's new compulsory insurance, fill in
arrival forms for South Africa, Taiwan and India before you fly, and if you're
travelling to China or South Korea in January, check whether their
visa-free and K-ETA exemptions have been extended.

## Changed in 2026

**Kenya: compulsory travel health insurance since 5 October.** Most visitors
staying under 12 months must have Kenyan Government-provided health
insurance. You buy it as part of the eTA application, or through the
eCitizen portal if you already have an eTA. Your own travel insurance
doesn't replace it. It's valid for 90 days and multiple entries.
[Kenya entry requirements](/destinations/kenya)

**Thailand: visa-free stays cut to 30 days.** Since 15 September, the
visa-exempt stay for tourists is 30 days, down from 60. You also need the
Thailand Digital Arrival Card, filed online within 72 hours before arrival.
[Thailand entry requirements](/destinations/thailand)

**South Africa: an online traveller declaration.** Since 1 July, everyone
entering or leaving South Africa must submit an online traveller declaration
before travelling. [South Africa entry requirements](/destinations/south-africa)

**Arrival cards elsewhere.** Taiwan's arrival card (TWAC) is filed online
within 3 days before you arrive. India requires an e-Arrival Card, which you
can complete up to 72 hours before landing; it isn't a visa.
[Taiwan](/destinations/taiwan) · [India](/destinations/india)

**Australia: no nicotine pouches.** Since 24 July, nicotine pouches can't be
brought into Australia, even with a prescription. The Australian Border Force
seizes them. [Nicotine rules for Australia](/bring/tobacco/australia)

**United Kingdom: a separate vaping allowance.** You can now bring up to
50 ml of vaping liquid duty-free. Above that, tax and duty are due on all of
it, not just the extra. [Vape rules for the UK](/bring/e-cigarettes/united-kingdom)

**Hong Kong: vapes in public.** Bringing e-cigarettes or heated tobacco into
Hong Kong was already banned. Since 30 April, possessing them in a public
place is an offence too. [Vape rules for Hong Kong](/bring/e-cigarettes/hong-kong)

## Deadlines to watch

**United States, 12 November and 11 December: hemp CBD.** Under federal law,
hemp CBD with no more than 0.3% delta-9 THC isn't marijuana. From 12 November,
products with cannabinoids a cannabis plant can't produce naturally no longer
count as hemp; from 11 December the rest of the new definition applies,
including a cap of 0.4 mg total THC per container.
[CBD rules for the US](/bring/cannabis/united-states)

**China, 31 December: the visa-free policy's end date.** China's Foreign
Ministry says its unilateral visa-free policy, which lets many passports stay
up to 30 days, runs until 31 December 2026. If it isn't extended, those
travellers will need a visa again. [China entry requirements](/destinations/china)

**South Korea, 31 December: the K-ETA exemption.** Twelve of the passports we
cover, including the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Japan, France and Germany,
don't need a K-ETA until 31 December 2026 (Korean time). After that, unless
it's extended, you'll need one before departure.
[South Korea entry requirements](/destinations/south-korea)

**Mongolia, end of 2026: the 30-day tourist exemption.** Mongolia's temporary
visa exemption for tourists from 34 countries runs until the end of 2026.
[Mongolia entry requirements](/destinations/mongolia)

We'll update each page as soon as an official source confirms an extension or
a change. The [change log](/changelog) lists every update, with its source.
