"Your passport must be valid for six months." You've probably heard that rule. But six
months from when? From the day you arrive, or from the day you leave? On a three-week
trip, that's a three-week difference in how long your passport needs to last.

Countries say it differently, and the wording matters. Here's how the rules compare in
the countries we've checked.

## Why the reference date matters

Take a trip from **1 to 20 November 2026**. Your passport must still be valid on:

- 6 months from arrival: **1 May 2027**
- 6 months after you leave: **20 May 2027**
- 3 months from arrival: **1 February 2027**
- 3 months after you leave: **20 February 2027**
- 60 days after your stay: **19 January 2027**
- for the whole stay: **20 November 2026**

## 6 months from the day you arrive

The most common rule, in [Egypt](/destinations/egypt), [Indonesia](/destinations/indonesia),
[Jordan](/destinations/jordan), [Kenya](/destinations/kenya), [Peru](/destinations/peru),
[Singapore](/destinations/singapore), [Sri Lanka](/destinations/sri-lanka),
[Taiwan](/destinations/taiwan) and [Vietnam](/destinations/vietnam).

## 6 months after you leave

- [Philippines](/destinations/philippines): at least 6 months beyond your intended stay.

## 3 months

- [Israel](/destinations/israel): at least three months from the date of arrival.
- **The Schengen Area**: at least 3 months after the date you intend to leave, and issued
  within the last 10 years. This comes from the Schengen Borders Code.
- [New Zealand](/destinations/new-zealand): at least 3 months after the date you plan to
  leave.
- [Montenegro](/destinations/montenegro): at least 3 months after your planned departure,
  and issued within the last 10 years.
- [Albania](/destinations/albania): Albania's own visa rules say at least 3 months after
  the date you leave, and issued within the last 10 years.

## 60 days after your stay

- [Turkey](/destinations/turkey): at least 60 days beyond the length of your stay, under
  Law No. 6458, Article 7.1(b).

## Valid for the whole stay

- [United Kingdom](/destinations/united-kingdom), [Chile](/destinations/chile) and
  [Colombia](/destinations/colombia): your passport must be valid for the whole of your
  stay.

## When the rule doesn't say from when

Some official sources only say "valid for at least 6 months" without saying from which
date. In those cases, we don't guess. Count from the day you leave, and you'll meet
either reading.

## Check your own dates

[Trip Check](/trip-check) and each country's entry-requirements page now include a
passport check. Enter your arrival date, departure date and passport expiry date, and it
tells you whether your passport meets the rule. The dates stay in your browser.
