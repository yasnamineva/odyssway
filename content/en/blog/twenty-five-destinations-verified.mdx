Five more destinations verified — Indonesia, Saudi Arabia, Brazil,
Singapore, and South Korea — brings Trip Check to 25 destinations covered,
plus the Schengen Area. This batch had the widest range of "same word,
different rule" surprises yet, and one case where the honest answer was
that we couldn't confirm the number everyone else publishes.

## Brazil brought back its visa requirement — for three nationalities, not all of them

Effective 10 April 2025, Brazil reinstated visa requirements for US,
Canadian, and Australian citizens, ending the waiver those nationalities
had held since 2019. It's explicitly reciprocal: the US requires visas
from Brazilian citizens, so Brazil now requires one back. UK citizens
weren't touched by this — their reciprocal waiver stayed in place, still
good for 90 days per entry, extendable once, capped at 180 days total in
any 12-month period.

## Singapore doesn't publish the number everyone quotes

This is the one we're proudest of, in a way that isn't flattering to the
rest of the travel-info industry. "US citizens get 90 days visa-free in
Singapore" is repeated everywhere online. We went looking for the
government document that actually says so. There isn't one. Singapore's
own ICA and its embassy in Washington both confirm US passport holders
don't need a visa — but the length of stay is set by the immigration
officer at entry, with no published per-nationality table. We re-checked
specifically because this figure mattered enough to double back on
(2026-09-10). We're publishing "not fixed — set at entry," not the 90
days everyone else copies, because that's what the source actually
supports. Canadians, by contrast, get an explicit government-confirmed
30 days: "Tourist visa: not required for stays of up to 30 days."

## Saudi Arabia's e-visa comes with insurance already included

The tourist e-visa — 1-year validity, multiple entry, up to 90 days per
visit within a rolling 365-day window — bundles mandatory travel health
insurance automatically, up to SAR 100,000 of coverage, into the visa
itself. You're not buying it separately; it's priced into the
application.

## Indonesia's visa-on-arrival has a hard ceiling

30 days initial stay, one extension of 30 more days at a local
immigration office (60 days total), and that's it — it cannot be
converted into a different stay permit. IDR 500,000, payable on arrival
or prepaid online through the eVOA portal.

## South Korea's biggest exemption has a deadline before the year is out

K-ETA, Korea's electronic travel authorization, has been temporarily
waived for a long list of nationalities — including the US, UK, and
Canada — since April 2023. We confirmed independently, via each
government's own travel advisory, that the current waiver runs through
31 December 2026. After that, K-ETA becomes mandatory again unless the
exemption is extended a second time. Canadians also get a longer
standard stay than most: 180 days, versus 90 for most other
nationalities, under a separate reciprocal agreement.
