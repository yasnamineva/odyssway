Retatrutide is a weight-loss peptide sold online by "research" sellers,
sometimes alongside tirzepatide. It isn't an approved medicine
in the US, Canada or New Zealand. At the border, that difference matters a
lot.

**Approved pens like Ozempic, Wegovy and Mounjaro can usually come with you.
Retatrutide usually can't.**

## Same kind of drug, different legal status

Semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy) and tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound) are
approved prescription medicines. Customs treats them like other prescribed
medicine: bring a sensible supply and proof it's yours.

Retatrutide isn't approved. So customs treats it as an **unapproved drug**,
whatever the vial's label says.

## Country by country

**United States.** Brand-name GLP-1 pens are FDA-approved, so visitors can
bring about a **90-day supply** with a prescription or a doctor's letter.
Retatrutide is not approved, and the FDA says importing an unapproved drug
is illegal, even for personal use. In March 2026 the FDA sent a warning
letter to a seller of retatrutide, saying its "Research Use Only" labels
didn't change the fact that it was selling drugs for human use.

**Canada.** GLP-1 pens are ordinary prescription drugs: a 90-day supply or
one course of treatment, whichever is less, in labelled packaging. Health
Canada's April 2026 advisory lists retatrutide among unauthorized peptides
it has seized, and says unauthorized drug products are illegal in Canada.

**New Zealand.** Medsafe names retatrutide among peptides that are
prescription medicines, and says it's unlawful to import them without
authorisation. A prescribed medicine can come with you: up to 3 months'
supply, with your prescription or a doctor's letter, declared on arrival.

## Compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide

There's a middle ground: compounded or non-brand versions of approved drugs.
For the US, our data notes that these aren't FDA-approved products, so they
can fall under the stricter unapproved-drug rules and should expect more
scrutiny than a brand-name pen.

## Before you pack

- **Brand-name pen, prescribed to you:** bring the prescription, keep it in
  the pharmacy packaging, and stay within the supply limit.
- **Vial bought online:** if it's retatrutide or another unapproved peptide,
  leave it at home for the US, Canada and New Zealand.
