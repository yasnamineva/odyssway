# How we verify what we publish

Travel rules published wrongly can cost you a fine or an entry ban, so this
page describes exactly how our information is produced — including its limits.

## Source policy

Every rule, date, fee or threshold on this site must be checked against a
**primary official source** before publication:

- EU law as published on EUR-Lex / the Official Journal (regulations, Council
  decisions)
- European Commission and other EU institution pages (europa.eu)
- National government authorities

Blogs, news articles and other travel sites are never used as the basis of a
value — at most they help us locate the primary source. Where two official
pages disagree, the legally controlling text (the regulation) wins; we record
the conflict. This has already caught real discrepancies — an official EU
information page still displaying the superseded €7 ETIAS fee after
[Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1411](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32025R1411)
set it to €20.

## Structured, dated, cited

Rules live in structured data files, each carrying the legal source, a
per-claim citation list, and the date it was last checked. Pages display
"Checked against official sources" with that date. If we can't reach a primary
source for a fact, we don't publish the fact — you may occasionally see a
gap where other sites confidently state a number; that's deliberate.

## Automated freshness checks

The official pages we rely on are monitored weekly for content changes. When
a watched source changes, the affected values are re-checked and their
"checked" dates updated.

## Verification is automated — here's what that means

Checks against official sources are performed with AI assistance and recorded
per claim; there is no separate human editorial review. That's why every value
links its source: you can — and for high-stakes decisions should — verify the
original yourself. The calculator additionally reproduces the European
Commission's own worked examples in an automated test suite, and its results
always link the [official EU calculator](https://ec.europa.eu/assets/home/visa-calculator/calculator.htm?lang=en)
for cross-checking.

## Found an error?

The fastest way to get it fixed is to point us at the official source that
contradicts us. Errors are corrected with priority over everything else.
