# About

Odyssway is a compliance toolkit for non-EU travellers to the Schengen Area:
a [90/180-day calculator](/calculator) built to match the EU's official
methodology, plain-language [rule guides](/rules/90-180-rule), and
[EES](/ees) resources for the digital-borders era.

## What makes it different

- **Edge cases done right.** Non-Schengen EU countries, historic accession
  dates (a 2024 trip to Bulgaria counts differently than a 2026 one),
  residence-permit rules — the cases generic calculators ignore.
- **Verified against official sources.** Every rule we publish is checked
  against a primary official source — EU regulations, Council decisions,
  European Commission pages — and cited. See [how we verify](/methodology)
  and the [full source list](/sources).
- **Tested against the EU's own examples.** Our calculation engine reproduces
  every worked example in the European Commission's official calculator manual
  in its automated test suite.
- **Private by design.** The calculator runs entirely in your browser. Your
  dates are never sent to our servers; shareable links encode them in the URL
  itself.

## What this site is not

We are not affiliated with the European Union, and nothing here is legal
advice or an official determination of your right to stay. Border authorities
decide. For personal legal questions, consult the competent national
authorities or a qualified adviser.
